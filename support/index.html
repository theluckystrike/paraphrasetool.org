Why I ask for tips

I am Michael Lip. I build paraphrasetool.org on my own. I started it in June 2026. It now has 18 free writing tools, including a sentence rephraser, an academic paraphraser, a paragraph spinner and a readability checker. They run in your browser, with no account.

What it costs

The domain renewal each year, and my hours to fix bugs and grow the word lists. Hosting is free on GitHub Pages. Some pages show Google ads.

Where tips go

First the domain. Then time to improve the synonym lists and fix what breaks.
